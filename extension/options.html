Cussijn Tree View

Draws your mailbox as a treemap, the way old SequoiaView drew disk usage. Each folder is a rectangle, sized by message count (or size) and colored by whichever tag shows up most inside it. The shading itself borrows the "cushion treemap" technique van Wijk & van de Wetering published at TU Eindhoven - where "Cussijn" (Dutch for cushion) comes from.

Open it with Ctrl+Shift+Y, from Tools -> Cussijn Tree View, or by right-clicking a folder and choosing "Open in Cussijn Tree View." Click any rectangle to drill in - first by whichever dimension you're grouping by, then by sender.

It only touches Thunderbird's own messenger.accounts, messenger.messages, and messenger.menus - reading headers and tags, and adding one context-menu entry. No network connection, nothing sent anywhere, no companion process. Nothing here to configure.
